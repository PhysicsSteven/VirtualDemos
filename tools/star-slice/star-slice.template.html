    Key

    Convection is drawn as tracer streaks circling counter rotating roll cells. Their extent comes from MESA's mixing_type and their relative speed from log_conv_vel (compressed so slow and fast regions both show); the roll pattern itself is illustrative, not computed. Overshoot shows as fainter, slower streaks; semiconvection as drifting layers; thermohaline mixing as thin radial fingers.

    Glow marks zones with εnuc above 10 erg g⁻¹ s⁻¹ and above 10⁻⁴ of the moment's peak. Channel shares use MESA's pp, cno and tri_alpha columns; any remainder is other reactions.

    Data notes

    Line of evidence: every number and every layer color on this page comes from the MESA profiles; the convective motion, element textures, granulation, sparkle and glow pulse are illustrative.
