      Fluids: Pressure, Buoyancy and Flow

      Drag a pressure probe down a tank, drop blocks that float or sink, weigh one in an overflow can like Archimedes, see an iceberg float 89% under, watch a Venturi tube trade pressure for speed, and aim Torricelli's jet.

      Falling Ball Viscometer

      Time balls falling through glycerol, oil or water and measure viscosity with Stokes' law. Then find where it fails: tube walls and fast, high Reynolds number flow.

      In Physics I

      OpenStax 1:6.4 1:14.7

    • Lab

      Terminal Velocity

      Jump with the textbook's skydiver and watch the drag grow until it matches the weight. Switch between spread eagle and head first, open the parachute, jump from 10 km where the air is thin, or drop stacks of coffee filters, and measure the terminal speed.

      In Physics I

      Granular Flow: Sorting and Landslides

      Sand of several kinds you choose (number, size, color, roughness, shape, material) in a rotating drum, a shaken box, a shear box, a landslide and a moving sand picture in liquid. Watch the sizes sort: small grains in the core of the drum, the Brazil nut rising, big stones on top of a slide.
