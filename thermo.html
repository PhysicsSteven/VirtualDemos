      Temperature and Heat

      Join a hot block to cold water and watch them reach one temperature; calibrate a gas thermometer and extrapolate to absolute zero; measure a rod's expansion and water's odd behavior near 4 °C; and probe the temperature through a layered wall.

    • Lab

      States of Matter

      Heat a crystal of argon atoms under a piston and watch the bonds between them break: the temperature holds still while it melts and again while it boils, because the heat goes into the bonds. A beaker view heats ice to steam with the textbook's specific and latent heats.

      • 2:1.4 Heat Transfer, Specific Heat, and Calorimetry
      • 2:1.5 Phase Changes

      Open demo Worksheet 1: Heating Curve and Latent Heat Worksheet 2: Bonds, Temperature and Latent Heat Notes

      Blackbody Radiation

      Explore thermal emission across temperatures, test Planck’s spectrum, Wien’s displacement law and Stefan–Boltzmann scaling, and connect the results to visible color.

    The kinetic theory of gases

    Vol 2 · Ch. 24 of 4 sections with an activity

    • Demo

      Gas in a Box

      Watch pressure and temperature emerge from molecules hitting the walls, speeds relax to Maxwell-Boltzmann, and a moving piston heat the gas. Then let two gases mix through a divider: measure diffusion from the mean square displacement and test Graham's law through a small hole.

      • 2:2.1 Molecular Model of an Ideal Gas
      • 2:2.2 Pressure, Temperature, and RMS Speed
      • 2:2.3 Heat Capacity and Equipartition of Energy
      • 2:2.4 Distribution of Molecular Speeds

      Open demo Worksheet 1: Gas in a Box Worksheet 2: Diffusion and Graham's Law Notes

    • Lab

      Heat Capacities of Gases

      Heat a gas with its piston locked, then free: the same heat warms it less when it pushes the piston. Measure C_V, C_P and γ, tell an atom from a molecule, and find γ again from a ball bouncing on a flask of gas.

      • 2:2.3 Heat Capacity and Equipartition of Energy
