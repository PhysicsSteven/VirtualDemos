What students should come away with

  • The four circuit demos live together on one page, one tab each. Each tab has its own teaching notes and questions, linked below.

Before class

Pick the tab for the day's topic and open its notes. Links to a single tab can use the page address with #dc, #rc, #ac or #transients at the end.

Running it in class

  1. DC circuits (Kirchhoff's rules, series and parallel, meters): see the notes for DC Circuit Builder.
  2. RC charging (time constant, energy): see the notes for RC Circuits.
  3. AC and resonance (phasors, impedance, resonance and Q): see the notes for AC Circuits and Resonance.
  4. Transients (RL decay, LC oscillation, damped RLC): GPT's laboratory page; classroom notes are still to be written.
