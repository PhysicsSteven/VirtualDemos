# Rutherford Scattering — Student Laboratory

[Open simulation](https://physicssteven.github.io/VirtualDemos/demos/rutherford.html)

**Research goal:** Test the relationship b=(k Z z e²/(2E))cot(theta/2).

## Procedure
1. Identify the mode, controls, observable and fixed parameters; write a prediction.
2. Record at least five settings of one independent variable and measure the response.
3. Repeat measurements if the result is noisy. Record units and uncertainty.
4. Plot data, fit the model, and compute a physical parameter.
5. Test a limiting case and discuss model assumptions.
