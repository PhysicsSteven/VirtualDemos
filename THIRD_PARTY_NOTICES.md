## Data

- **NIST Atomic Spectra Database and Handbook of Basic Atomic Spectroscopic Data** (Spectra Explorer, Franck–Hertz). U.S. government work, public domain. https://physics.nist.gov
- **ENSDF decay data via NNDC NuDat 3** (Gamma Spectroscopy). National Nuclear Data Center, Brookhaven National Laboratory. https://www.nndc.bnl.gov
- **AME2020 atomic masses** (Nuclear Binding Energy). M. Wang et al., Chinese Physics C 45, 030003 (2021).
- **pyroomacoustics materials database** (absorption coefficients in Room Acoustics). LCAV, EPFL. MIT License, Copyright © EPFL LCAV. https://github.com/LCAV/pyroomacoustics
- **MESA** (Modules for Experiments in Stellar Astrophysics). The Star Slice profiles were computed by the author with MESA; MESA itself is not included. https://docs.mesastar.org
- **NASA Blue Marble and Earth at Night images** (the Earth in the Units, Scales and Estimates zoom, embedded in `demos/units.html` at reduced size) and in `demos/lib/earth.js` for 3D views. NASA Earth Observatory, public domain (NASA images are not copyrighted). Copies taken from the example images of the three-globe project (vasturiano/three-globe on GitHub). https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov
- **JPL approximate planetary elements** (E. M. Standish, JPL Solar System Dynamics) for the planet positions in the same zoom. https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html
- Other constants and measured values are cited where they are used in each demo and its notes.
