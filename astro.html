      Rainbows

      Rotating Frames

      Open demo Notes

    • Demo

      Gyroscope Precession

      Units, Scales and Estimates

      Weigh a planet's iron core from its mean density and moment of inertia, watch a mantle churn into moving plates or under one stiff lid, and float continents on the mantle.

      Open demo Worksheet: Inside a Planet (Astro 2203) Notes

    • Lab

      Fluids: Pressure, Buoyancy and Flow
