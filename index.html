    Courses

    • Physics I

      Mechanics and oscillations · 10 units · 30 demos

      Measurement and vectors through oscillations, in the order of the course schedule. OpenStax University Physics Volume 1.

    • Physics II

      Electricity and magnetism · 14 units · 34 demos

      Charges and fields through Maxwell’s equations and electromagnetic waves, then AC circuits, light and geometric optics, and wave optics. OpenStax University Physics Volume 2, and Volume 3 for optics.

    • Astro 2203

      Solar system astronomy · 10 weeks · 3 tests · 28 demos

      From naked eye observations of the sky to the planets, their moons, the small bodies and planets around other stars, week by week through the Winter Quarter, with a review before each of the three tests and one required worksheet most weeks. The worksheets are written for an introductory class: algebra, ratios and short graphs.

    Modules that follow the textbook chapters

    • Acoustics

      Waves and sound · 3 units · 14 demos

      Waves on strings, the speed of sound, resonance, musical tones and the Doppler effect, then two demos beyond the textbook. OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, chapters 16 and 17.

    • Thermodynamics

      Heat and the laws of thermodynamics · 5 units · 8 demos

      Temperature and heat, phase changes, the kinetic theory of gases, and the first and second laws with engines and entropy. OpenStax University Physics Volume 2, chapters 1 to 4.

    • Modern Physics

      Relativity, quanta, atoms and nuclei · 7 units · 36 demos

      Relativity, photons and matter waves, quantum mechanics, atoms, solids, nuclei, particles and cosmology. OpenStax University Physics Volume 3, chapters 5 to 11.

    • Astronomy

      Sky, planets, stars and galaxies · 6 units · 35 demos

      Every astronomy demo on the site: the sky from Earth, gravitation and orbits, telescopes and light, how stars shine and age, galaxies and the expanding universe, and the planets and their surfaces. Each card lists the Astro 2203 introductory worksheets too. OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, chapter 13, and Volume 3, chapters 10 and 11, where they apply.

    • Medical Physics

      Radiation physics and proton therapy · 9 units · 18 demos

      For the junior and senior medical physics track, beside the radiation physics class and ahead of the seniors’ lab at the proton therapy center: sources and decay, photon interactions, charged particles in matter, dosimetry, radiobiology, shielding, proton therapy and imaging. Calculus throughout; the upper level worksheets read cited data tables and carry uncertainties, and the other worksheets on each card work as warm ups.
