Virtual Physics Demos

Interactive demonstrations for introductory physics. Each one runs in the browser with nothing to install.

Astronomy

  • Blackbody Radiation Slide a temperature from a human body to a blue supergiant and watch the Planck curve, Wien's peak and the star's color change.
    Planck curve · Wien's law · Stefan-Boltzmann · star colors

Modern physics

  • Photoelectric Effect Shine light on a metal, find the stopping voltage, and measure Planck's constant from the slope, the way Millikan did in 1916.
    photons · work function · stopping voltage · Planck's constant

Mechanics

  • Projectile Range Launch a baseball, a ping pong ball or a cannonball with and without air resistance, and see why the best launch angle drops below 45°.
    projectile motion · quadratic drag · terminal speed

Thermodynamics

  • Gas in a Box Watch pressure and temperature emerge from molecules hitting the walls, speeds relax to Maxwell-Boltzmann, and a moving piston heat the gas.
    kinetic theory · ideal gas law · adiabatic compression

Electricity and magnetism

  • Coulomb Sandbox Drag point charges and watch the field lines, equipotentials and potential update live, as a 2D map or a rotatable 3D landscape.
    electric field · potential · superposition

Optics

  • Optics Bench Drag lenses, mirrors, prisms and light sources. Rays bend and bounce live, and images form where they cross.
    thin lens equation · mirrors · total internal reflection · dispersion
