Barrier for a PET uptake room

Uses the ¹⁸F source's activity A per patient and the distance r from the patient to the occupied spot beyond the wall.

More about this

Weekly dose with no barrier: N patients × the dose from each while it decays for t hours in the room, D = Ḋ₀ τ (1 − e−t/τ), times 0.36 for a patient. The barrier must transmit B = P/(T D), with P the shielding design goal (NCRP Report 147: 0.1 mSv a week for a controlled area, 0.02 for an uncontrolled one) and T the occupancy factor of the space beyond (NCRP Report 147, Table 4.1). The broad beam thickness inverts the TG-108 fit; the narrow beam thickness, ln(1/B)/μ, leaves out the scattered photons and comes out too thin.
