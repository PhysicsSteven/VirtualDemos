Electrostatics at Work

Three machines that run on electric charge: a Van de Graaff generator charging its dome until the air breaks down, an ink jet printer steering charged drops onto paper, and a smokestack precipitator pulling charged dust out of a gas flow.

Printer

More about this

Plates 10.0 mm long and 2.0 mm apart; the paper is 15.0 mm past the end of the plates. The lower plate is at the voltage set, the upper one grounded, so the field points up and positive drops bend up. Print PHYS works out the charge for each dot from the settings, as the printer's controller does.

Precipitator

More about this

One channel: a charging wire midway between two grounded plates 0.250 m apart, so s = 0.125 m from wire to plate.
