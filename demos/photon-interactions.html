Photon Interactions in Matter

A photon crossing matter is scattered coherently, scattered by an electron (Compton), absorbed by an atom (photoelectric) or turned into an electron and a positron (pair production). Read each process's share of the attenuation coefficient from the NIST tables for any element or tissue from 1 keV to 100 MeV, see which one wins at each energy and atomic number, send a beam through a slab to see narrow and broad beam attenuation and buildup, and harden an X-ray beam to measure its half value layers.

The model and its formulas

μ/ρ = σ_coh/ρ + σ_incoh/ρ + τ/ρ + κ_n/ρ + κ_e/ρ, a compound: Σ wᵢ (μ/ρ)ᵢ

μ = (μ/ρ) ρ, narrow beam: I/I₀ = e^(−μt), HVL = ln 2 / μ, mean free path 1/μ

per atom: σₐ = (μ/ρ) A / N_A; per electron: σₑ = σₐ / Z

μ_tr/ρ = τ/ρ (1 − δ/hν) + σ_incoh/ρ ⟨T⟩/hν + κ/ρ (1 − 2mc²/hν)

μ_en/ρ = μ_tr/ρ (1 − g)

broad beam: K/K₀ = B e^(−μt), B ≥ 1 the buildup factor

air kerma K = Σ Φ(E) E μ_en/ρ(air, E); HVL₁ halves K, HVL₂ halves it again; h = HVL₁/HVL₂

Where the numbers come from
  • Cross sections: NIST XCOM (Berger, Hubbell, Seltzer, Chang, Coursey, Sukumar, Zucker and Olsen, Standard Reference Database 8, doi:10.18434/T48G6X), per element Z = 1 to 92, standard energy grid with the absorption edges.
  • μ/ρ and μ_en/ρ tables, densities and compositions of the tissues and shields: Hubbell and Seltzer, NIST Standard Reference Database 126 (doi:10.18434/T4D01F), Tables 1 to 4; tissues from ICRU Report 44. XCOM sums for water and air match its μ/ρ to 0.05 %.
  • Atomic form factors and incoherent scattering functions: Hubbell et al. 1975, J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data 4, 471. K and L3 fluorescence yields: Krause 1979, J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data 8, 307. X-ray line energies: Deslattes et al. 2003, Rev. Mod. Phys. 75, 35. All as tabulated in xraylib 4.3 (Schoonjans et al. 2011, Spectrochim. Acta B 66, 776).
  • Energy transfer: F. H. Attix, Introduction to Radiological Physics and Radiation Dosimetry (Wiley, 1986), chapter 7. Klein–Nishina cross section; mc² = 510.99895069 keV, r_e, N_A and hc from CODATA 2022.
  • Sampling: coherent as in PENELOPE (Salvat, PENELOPE 2014, NEA/NSC/DOC(2015)3); Klein–Nishina as in Geant4's G4KleinNishinaCompton.
  • X-ray spectra: SpekPy 2.5.4 (Poludniowski et al. 2021, Med. Phys. 48, 3630), tungsten anode at 12°, 0.8 mm beryllium window. Its half value layers agree with the ones computed here to 0.2 %.
  • Gamma energies: ¹³⁷Cs 661.657 keV; ⁶⁰Co 1173.228 and 1332.492 keV, here one beam at their mean, 1252.86 keV (ENSDF).
