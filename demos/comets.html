Comets and Meteor Showers

Bring a real comet in from the cold on its orbit from JPL and watch it grow two tails that point away from the Sun, not behind it: a straight ion tail blown by the solar wind and a curved dust tail pushed by sunlight. Then follow the dust it leaves along its orbit, and watch Earth plow through that stream on the same date every year.

The model and its formulas

a = (q + Q)/2, e = (Q − q)/(Q + q), P = a^1.5 years (a in au)

dust: sunlight pushes out with β times the Sun's pull, β = 3L/(16π G M c ρ s)

ion tail along the solar wind as the comet feels it: v_wind r̂ − v_comet

shower date: when Earth's longitude is where the comet's orbit passes closest to Earth's orbit
