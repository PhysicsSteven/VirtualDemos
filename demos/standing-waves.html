More about this

y₁ = A₁ sin(k₁x − ω₁t), y₂ = A₂ sin(k₂x + ω₂t), both at v = 2 m/s. With A₁ ≠ A₂ the sum is a standing wave of amplitude 2·min(A) plus a traveling wave of amplitude |A₁ − A₂|. The standing wave ratio is the largest over the smallest amplitude along x, (A₁ + A₂)/|A₁ − A₂|.

More about this

y = A₁ cos(2πf₁t) + A₂ cos(2πf₂t). The loudness envelope is √(A₁² + A₂² + 2A₁A₂ cos 2π(f₂ − f₁)t), which repeats every 1/|f₁ − f₂|. The top two strips zoom in on the moment at the playhead.
