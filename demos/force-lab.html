The model and its formulas

F = G m₁m₂ / r² F = k q₁q₂ / r²

Model: two uniform spheres, or two point charges, at rest; r is the distance between their centers (a uniform sphere pulls as if its mass were at its center). The force on each is the same size and opposite in direction (Newton's third law). Gravity: G = 6.674 30 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² (CODATA 2022); the spheres are lead, 11 340 kg/m³, so a sphere's radius is (3m/4πρ)^(1/3) unless Same size is on. Coulomb: k = 1/(4πε₀) = 8.987 551 786 2 × 10⁹ N·m²/C² (CODATA 2022), e = 1.602 176 634 × 10⁻¹⁹ C (exact); the charges are points drawn as balls, and two balls may not overlap. Nothing else acts: no Earth, no air, no other charges. Positions are read on the ruler to 0.1 of its unit.
