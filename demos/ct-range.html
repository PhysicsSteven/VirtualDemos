The model and its sources

HU = 1000 (μ/μ_water − 1)

μ = ρ N_A Σ (wᵢ/Aᵢ)(Zᵢ K_KN + Zᵢ^2.86 K_coh + Zᵢ^4.62 K_ph) (Schneider et al. 1996)

RSP = S_tissue/S_water (Bethe; I from NIST, mixtures by Bragg's rule)

R_plan = WET (1 + a) + b, a = 3.5 %, b = 1 mm (Paganetti 2012)

Range uncertainty budget (Paganetti 2012)
Sources of range uncertainty

H. Paganetti, Phys. Med. Biol. 57, R99 (2012), Table 2, as he presented it (AAPM CAMPS, Yale, 2012): estimated uncertainties of the range in a patient for treatment planning with an analytical dose calculation, each at 1.5 standard deviations. The percentages add in quadrature to 2.7 % + 1.2 mm without the last line and 4.6 % + 1.2 mm with it; with Monte Carlo dose calculation the lateral inhomogeneity term nearly vanishes and the total is about 2.4 % + 1.2 mm. Margins of 3.5 % + 1 mm come from this kind of sum.
