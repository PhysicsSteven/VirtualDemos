Inside the Sun

Why the Sun neither explodes nor collapses, what it burns and how fast, how long its light takes to get out, and how neutrinos let us see its core today. Squeeze the core and watch it push back; weigh four hydrogens against one helium; follow a photon's random walk; race a neutrino out of the core.

The model and its formulas

thermostat: E = −¾ GM²/R (n = 3 polytrope, virial theorem), dE/dt = Lfusion − L☉

Lfusion ∝ ρc Tc−2/3 e−τ, τ = 3 (EG/4kTc)1/3, Tc ∝ 1/R, ρc ∝ 1/R³

4 ¹H → ⁴He: Δm = 4 m(¹H) − m(⁴He), E = Δm c²; lifetime t = 0.10 × 0.0071 × M c²/L

random walk: distance ≈ √N ℓ, so N = (R/ℓ)², t = Nℓ/c = R²/(ℓc)
