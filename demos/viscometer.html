Blind hides η, the Stokes prediction, the Reynolds number and the fitted η, leaving the timer and the densities. The Mystery liquid always hides its η.

Model: drag F = 6πηrv × K(d/D) × C_D Re/24, with the standard drag curve of Clift, Grace and Weber (Oseen's 1 + 3Re/16 below Re = 0.01) and Haberman and Sayre's wall factor K for a sphere on the axis of a tube. The wall factor is a slow flow result and is applied at all Re as an approximation. The ball's motion includes buoyancy and the added mass ½ρ_f V. Densities in kg/m³: steel 7800, glass 2500, nylon 1140. Liquids at 20 °C: glycerol 1261 and η = 1.41 Pa·s; castor oil 961 and 0.986 Pa·s; motor oil (typical SAE 30) 875 and 0.29 Pa·s; water 998 and 1.00 mPa·s.
