Speeding Up on a Curve

Moving on a circle at changing speed: the acceleration has a part along the path, a_t = dv/dt, and a part toward the centre, a_c = v²/r. The total is no longer toward the centre, and friction must supply all of it.

g = 9.81 m/s²

Blind hides the accelerations, the angle and the predicted slip time, leaving the speed, the radius and the clock.

Model: a car as a point on a flat circular road, with a constant rate of change of speed; static friction supplies the whole horizontal force, up to μs mg. When it would need more, the car slides off along a straight line at the speed it had. Shown at real speed, so large radii look slow.
