Belousov–Zhabotinsky Reaction

A chemical clock. Stirred in a beaker, the solution flips from red to blue and back on a steady beat: a limit cycle, periodic and not chaotic. Left unstirred in a thin layer, it carries waves that form targets and spirals. Fed through a stirred flow reactor, it can turn chaotic.
