Shells, the Periodic Table and X-Rays

No two electrons in an atom can share all four quantum numbers, so they fill shells and subshells one place at a time, and the periodic table follows. Knock an electron out of the innermost shell and the atom gives off an X-ray whose energy grows as (Z − 1)².
