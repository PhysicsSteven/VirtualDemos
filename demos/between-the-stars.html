Between the Stars

Look through a lane of dust and watch a star dim and redden. Light a cloud with a hot star or a cooler one and see it glow red or blue, or stay dark. Then listen to cold hydrogen with its 21 cm radio line, which goes straight through the dust.

The model and its formulas

dimming: A_V = N_H / (2.21 × 10²¹ cm⁻²), N_H = n L; let through f = 10^(−0.4 A_λ)

A_λ/A_V from Cardelli, Clayton and Mathis (R_V = 3.1); for air, Rayleigh scattering τ ∝ λ⁻⁴ (Hansen and Travis), A_V = 1.0857 n L σ_V

distance if the dust is ignored: d_thought/d_true = 1/√f

ionizing photons Q = 4πR² · π∫ B_ν/(hν) dν above 13.6 eV; ionized radius R_S = (3Q/(4π n² α_B))^{1/3}

21 cm: f₀ = 1,420.405751768 MHz, λ = c/f₀, flips each second = (3/4) N A, A = 2.8843 × 10⁻¹⁵ s⁻¹

Doppler: λ = λ₀(1 + v/c), so f = f₀/(1 + v/c)
