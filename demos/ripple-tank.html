The model and its formulas

ω² = (g k + σk³/ρ) tanh(k d), λ = 2π/k, c = f λ

two sources d apart: bright lines at d sin θ = m λ, quiet (nodal) lines at d sin θ = (m + ½) λ

one slit of width a: quiet lines at a sin θ = m λ (m = 1, 2, …)

side view, a depth s down: half width a = A cosh k(d − s)/sinh kd, half height b = A sinh k(d − s)/sinh kd, b/a = tanh k(d − s)
