Two Speaker Interference

Two speakers play the same tone. Walk a microphone across the room and listen: it is loud where the sound from one speaker arrives a whole number of wavelengths after the other, and quiet halfway between. The quiet lines give the wavelength, and the wavelength the speed of sound.
