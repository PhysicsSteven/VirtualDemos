Craters, Cooling and Surface Ages

Drop an asteroid on the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Earth or Mars and see the crater it makes, from a small bowl to a basin with a ring of peaks. Count the craters on a surface to date it with the crater clock, and cool a Moon, a Mars and an Earth to see why small worlds go quiet first.

The asteroid and the target

Click or tap each crater at least as wide as the purple circle to mark it; click again to unmark.

All three start equally hot and are made of the same rock with the same radioactive elements. Only their size and gravity differ.
