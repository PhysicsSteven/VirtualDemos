Two cursors across the screen and the histogram, for measuring fringe positions. Drag a cursor, or press anywhere on the screen or the histogram to bring the nearer one there. Put them on two fringes of the same kind several spacings apart (dark fringes are not shifted by the envelope) and divide B − A by the number of spacings between them. The predicted pattern, its marks and the curve are hidden while the cursors are on.

Which slit detector

More about this

An idealized detector: when it fires, it records which slit the particle went through and does nothing else. That record alone removes the interference: those particles form the sum of two single slit patterns. It fires for a fraction η of the particles, so the pattern is η(I₁ + I₂) + (1 − η)I₁₂ and the fringe contrast drops to 1 − η.

More about this

Positions are y = L tan θ, measured from the center of the screen. The orders that land exactly on an envelope zero are missing. The falling envelope pulls each actual peak slightly toward the center, most visibly when d/a is small.

Intensity on the screen

The model and its formulas

I(θ) = I₀ · cos²(πd sin θ/λ) · [sin β/β]², β = πa sin θ/λ

The first factor is two slit interference: fringes spaced λL/d. The second is the diffraction of each slit: a broad envelope whose first zero sits at sin θ = λ/a. The pattern is their product, so the envelope sets how bright each fringe can be.

The model and its formulas

3D view: the slits are long, so each one sends out a cylindrical wave and the fringes are lines across the screen. The slits keep their true ratio of width to spacing. Drag the empty bench, or press [ and ], to turn it; the 3D view button or V switches to the top view.

Model: a monochromatic plane wave falls on two slits, and the screen pattern is the paraxial Fresnel calculation, which matches the formula above in the far field. Each hit is drawn at random from that pattern; its position along the slits is just spread out for display. Slits are drawn enlarged and L is not to scale. Hits count only if they land on the screen shown.
