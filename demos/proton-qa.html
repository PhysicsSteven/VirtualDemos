The model and its sources

R90: the depth past the peak where the dose falls to 90 % of the peak, read from a stack of chambers every 2 mm

σ(z)² = σ_air² + σ_MCS(z)² (spot size in air, then Fermi–Eyges scattering in water)

Γ = min √(r²/Δd² + δ²/ΔD²) over the plan; a point passes if Γ ≤ 1 (Low et al. 1998)

Tolerances
QA tolerances

Daily output, range and spot position: AAPM TG-224 (Arjomandy et al., Med. Phys. 46(8), e678, 2019). TG-224 sets no daily spot size check; ±10 % is the value used by the auto trending daily QA program of a ProBeam center (J. Appl. Clin. Med. Phys., 2021, PMC7856486). Patient QA: 3 %/3 mm with at least 95 % passing (Pidikiti et al. 2018, Proteus ONE), and 3 %/2 mm with 95 % passing and action below 90 % (AAPM TG-218, Miften et al., Med. Phys. 45(4), e53, 2018), both with a 10 % low dose threshold.
