The model and its formulas

density ρ = M / (4/3 π R³)

spin kept (I = k M R², same shape): P = P₀ (R/R₀)²

magnetic flux kept (B π R²): B = B₀ (R₀/R)²

angle θ from our line of sight to a pole: cos θ = cos α cos ζ + sin α sin ζ cos φ; a pulse while θ < ρ

pulse width W = 2 arccos[(cos ρ − cos α cos ζ)/(sin α sin ζ)]

spin down: Ė = 4π² I Ṗ/P³, τ = P/(2Ṗ), B = √(3μ₀c³ I P Ṗ/(32π³R⁶))
