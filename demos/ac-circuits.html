AC Circuits and Resonance

A sine wave source drives a resistor, an inductor and a capacitor in series. Each part shifts the current's timing in its own way, and at one frequency the inductor and capacitor cancel.

Model: ideal parts (no coil resistance, no capacitor leakage, no wire inductance), an ideal source with no internal resistance, and lumped elements. Steady state uses the exact phasor solution and applies at once when you change a value. Switch on solves L di/dt + Ri + q/C = V₀ sin ωt exactly from i = 0 and q = 0. With an ideal capacitor alone the current jumps at t = 0. Dot speed is proportional to i, scaled to the current axis.
