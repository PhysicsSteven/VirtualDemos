The model and its formulas

pressure P = n k T

Jeans mass M_J = (5kT/(Gμm_u))^{3/2} (3/(4πρ))^{1/2}

uniform cloud: R̈ = 5c²/R − GM/R², c² = kT/(μm_u)

spin kept: P = P₀ (D/D₀)²; disk where spin holds the gas: r = 4π²R₀⁴/(P₀²GM)

Wien: λ_peak = 2.898 × 10⁻³ m K / T

protostar core: P_c = ρ_c kT_c/(μ_ion m_u) + √[(ρ_c kT_c/(μ_e m_u))² + P_deg²]
