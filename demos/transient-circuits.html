Transient Circuits Laboratory

RC · RL · LC · RLC — ideal free response with analytic solutions

Original RC demo · Worksheet 2: RC Time Constant · Worksheet 3: RLC Oscillations

This laboratory models an initially energized circuit after the source is disconnected. In LC/RLC modes, a capacitor initially holds charge Q₀ and the inductor starts with zero current. In RL mode, an inductor initially carries I₀ and discharges through a resistor. In RC mode, a capacitor initially holds Q₀. The ideal model excludes stray resistance and parasitic capacitance.

Sign convention: Q is charge on the capacitor reference plate and I = dQ/dt. VC = Q/C, VR = RI, VL = L dI/dt, so VC + VR + VL = 0. For RL, positive I starts at I₀. Current is signed; initial capacitor discharge gives negative I.

Experiment

R = 0 is permitted for LC and RLC; RL with R = 0 has constant current and no decay. RLC with R = 0 is exactly periodic in this ideal model. Sampling is deterministic. RC requires R ≥ 0.1 Ω; zero resistance would produce an impulse current. Changing a circuit parameter resets the sample time.
