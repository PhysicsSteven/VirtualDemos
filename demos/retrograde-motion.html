The model and its formulas

synodic period S: 1/S = |1/P − 1/P⊕|, P⊕ = 365.256 d

lit fraction = (1 + cos α)/2, α the angle Sun–planet–Earth

angular size = 2 arcsin(R/Δ), R the planet's radius, Δ its distance
