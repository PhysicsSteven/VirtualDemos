Center of Mass

The centre of mass is the mass weighted average position. Find it for masses on a plate, then see that it keeps moving as if all the mass were there: a firework's pieces scatter but their centre of mass follows the same parabola, and walking on a boat cannot move the centre of mass of you and the boat.

g = 9.81 m/s²
