CT Image Reconstruction

A CT scanner never sees the inside of a patient: it measures how much each ray is weakened on its way through. Watch those measurements pile up into a sinogram, rebuild the image by backprojection, and see why the projections have to be filtered first. Then find out what the number of views, the angles covered, the detector bins and the photon count do to streaks, sharpness and noise.
