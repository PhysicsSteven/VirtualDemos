More about this

Transits measure the radius; radial velocity measures the mass times sin i. A transit also tells you i is near 90°, so together they give the true mass, and mass over volume gives the density: rock, water or gas.

The model and its formulas

Model: one planet and its star in a two body orbit. The planet's own light is ignored, so there is no secondary eclipse. Limb darkening is quadratic with fixed Sun like coefficients (u₁ = 0.40, u₂ = 0.26), so the true dip is a little deeper than (Rp/Rs)². The duration readout uses the standard approximation for eccentric orbits. Noise is white and Gaussian. In the top view the star and planet are drawn larger than scale.
