Keys: Tab or ] selects the next charge, Shift+Tab or [ the previous one. Arrow keys move the selected charge 0.1 cm, Shift+arrow 1 cm. + and − change its charge by 0.5 nC. Delete removes it. P switches between the probe and the last selected charge (Escape also switches to the probe); with the probe selected, arrow keys move it 0.1 cm, Shift+arrow 1 cm. R turns a selected rod, ring or disk by 15° (Shift+R the other way). C captures a reading. In the 3D landscape, A and D rotate the view, W and S tilt it, Z and X zoom in and out, 0 resets it.

The probe is inside a charge's 1.2 mm model ball. A true point charge has no finite V or E here; these values are for a uniformly charged ball.

More about this

Hover over the field, tap to place the probe, or press P on the field and use the arrow keys. The probe sits on a 0.1 cm grid. Each charge is modeled as a uniformly charged ball 1.2 mm in radius, inside its drawn disc; outside the ball V and E are exact point charge values.

Model: a rod, ring or disk carries its charge Q spread uniformly, and V and E are the exact integrals of k dq/r: closed forms for the rod, and complete elliptic integrals for the ring and the disk off their axis (on the axis these reduce to the textbook formulas). Within 1.2 mm of a body its charge is treated as a line or sheet of uniformly charged balls 1.2 mm in radius, integrated numerically to 1 part in 10¹⁰.
