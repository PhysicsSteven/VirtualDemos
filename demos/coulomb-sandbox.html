Coulomb Sandbox

Drag the charges around, and drag the knob on a selected charge to change its value. Switch to 3D to see the potential as a landscape of hills and wells.

1 grid square = 1 cm · q in nC · k = 8.99×10⁹ N·m²/C²
Blind

Keys: Tab or ] selects the next charge, Shift+Tab or [ the previous one. Arrow keys move the selected charge 0.1 cm, Shift+arrow 1 cm. + and − change its charge by 0.5 nC. Delete removes it. P switches between the probe and the last selected charge (Escape also switches to the probe); with the probe selected, arrow keys move it 0.1 cm, Shift+arrow 1 cm. C captures a reading. In the 3D landscape, A and D rotate the view, W and S tilt it, Z and X zoom in and out, 0 resets it.

The probe is inside a charge's 1.2 mm model ball. A true point charge has no finite V or E here; these values are for a uniformly charged ball.

Hover over the field, tap to place the probe, or press P on the field and use the arrow keys. The probe sits on a 0.1 cm grid. Each charge is modeled as a uniformly charged ball 1.2 mm in radius, inside its drawn disc; outside the ball V and E are exact point charge values.

Blind hides the field lines, the equipotentials, the potential shading, the 3D landscape and the field map data table. The charges, the grid and the probe stay, so students map the field from probe readings.
