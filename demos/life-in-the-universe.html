Life in the Universe

Squeeze Earth's 4.6 billion years into one class period and watch life arrive early and people arrive in the last second. Find where a star's habitable zone lies and whether the star lives long enough for complex life. Then multiply the Drake equation's factors, fill the Galaxy with civilizations, and see how far apart they would be.

The model and its formulas

time on the clock = (time ago ÷ Earth's age) × the whole clock, counted back from the end

habitable zone = (0.95 to 1.4 au) × √L, L ≈ M⁴, lifetime t ≈ 10 billion years × M/L = 10/M³

temperature with no greenhouse T = 255 K × L^¼ / √d

N = R* × fp × ne × fl × fi × fc × L, spacing = √(disk area ÷ N)
