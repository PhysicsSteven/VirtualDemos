Sign rule: curl the fingers of your right hand along the loop's direction; a current along your thumb counts as positive. For a counterclockwise loop that means out of the page. The loop color shows B·dl: green where B runs along the loop's direction, purple where it runs against it.

    Force between currents

    More about this

    Each wire sits in the field of the others and feels F/L = I × B per meter of its length. Parallel currents attract and opposite currents repel, with equal and opposite forces (Newton's third law).
