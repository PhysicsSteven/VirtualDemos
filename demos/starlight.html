Starlight: Brightness, Luminosity and Magnitudes

How much light a star gives off and how bright it looks are different things, and distance links them. Spread light over bigger and bigger squares, compare two stars, climb the magnitude ladder, line every star up at 10 parsecs, and count the stars near the Sun to see why the night sky is not a fair sample.
