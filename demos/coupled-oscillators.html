Model

Point masses on ideal massless linear springs (Hooke's law at every stretch), moving along one line. On the air track the masses glide without friction; their weight is carried by the air cushion and plays no part. Hanging, gravity is uniform. The springs are drawn relaxed 25 cm apart, center to center; block and coil sizes are drawing choices, and stiffer springs are drawn with thicker wire.

Without drag the motion is the exact sum of normal modes, evaluated at each instant, so energy stays fixed to rounding error. With drag the equations are integrated with fourth order Runge–Kutta at a step of at most 1/800 of the shortest mode period, and the energy readout checks that kinetic plus potential energy plus the work done against drag stays fixed. The modes are found by Cholesky reduction of M and Jacobi rotations.
