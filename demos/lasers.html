Lasers and Stimulated Emission

A photon can make an excited atom emit an exact copy of itself. Put enough excited atoms between two mirrors and the copies multiply into a laser beam, but only once the upper level holds more atoms than the lower one.

The model and its formulas

B₁₂ = B₂₁ (equal degeneracies)
absorption: B₁₂ρ(ν) N₁ · stimulated: B₂₁ρ(ν) N₂ · spontaneous: A₂₁ N₂

Pump light at the transition frequency drives absorption and stimulated emission at the same rate W per atom, so dN₂/dt = W(N₁ − N₂) − A₂₁N₂. However hard you pump, N₂ only approaches N₁: the medium becomes transparent, never amplifying. Lasers need a third or fourth level. Here kT ≪ hν is assumed for the pumped atom.

More about this

Ruby (Cr³⁺ in Al₂O₃): pumped into the broad ⁴T₂ and ⁴T₁ bands by green and violet light, which relax in well under a microsecond to the ²E level. ²E lives about 3 ms and lases to the ground state ⁴A₂ on the R₁ line, 694.3 nm. The lower level is the ground state, so more than half of all Cr³⁺ ions must be excited.

Nd:YAG (Nd³⁺ in Y₃Al₅O₁₂): pumped at 808 nm into ⁴F₅/₂, which decays quickly to ⁴F₃/₂ (lifetime about 230 µs). Lasing at 1064 nm ends on ⁴I₁₁/₂, about 2100 cm⁻¹ (0.26 eV) above the ground state. At 300 K the Boltzmann factor leaves only 4 × 10⁻⁵ of the ions there, and it empties in nanoseconds, so any excited ion is already an inversion.

Helium neon: electrons in the discharge excite helium to its metastable 2¹S₀ level (20.62 eV). A collision hands the energy to neon 3s₂ (Paschen notation; 2p⁵5s, 20.66 eV), the 0.05 eV gap made up by the atoms' kinetic energy. 3s₂ lases at 632.8 nm to 2p₄ (2p⁵3p, 18.70 eV), which decays fast to the 1s levels (2p⁵3s); those empty mainly by collisions with the tube wall, which is why the tube is narrow. Other lines: 3.39 µm (3s₂ to 3p₄) and 1.15 µm (2s₂ to 2p₄, fed from helium 2³S₁ at 19.82 eV).

Wavelengths are the usual values in air. Level energies: NIST Atomic Spectra Database (He, Ne); W. Koechner, Solid State Laser Engineering (ruby, Nd:YAG). Energy axes are to scale except across the break in the helium neon diagram.
