More about this

Typical energy per kilogram (higher heating values, which vary by sample): natural gas 55 MJ, gasoline 46 MJ, coal 27 MJ, dry wood 18 MJ, food fat 37.7 MJ and sugar 16.7 MJ (9 and 4 kcal/g), TNT 4.184 MJ (by definition), lithium ion cells 0.7 MJ (about 200 Wh/kg), uranium 235 fully fissioned 8.2 × 10⁷ MJ (200 MeV per fission). Hydro: gh per kg at 90% turbine efficiency. Wind: kinetic energy ½v² per kg of air through the rotor, at the Betz limit 16/27. Solar: 20% panel efficiency at the average sunlight you set (it depends on place and season).
