Telescope Aberrations

A perfect telescope turns a star into a tiny Airy disk. A real one blurs it: the mirror's shape, a star far from the center, a lens that bends colors differently, or the moving air above. Each blur leaves its own fingerprint. Work out what is wrong, then fix it.
