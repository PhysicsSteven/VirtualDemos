Capture stores L, θ₀ and the last full period from the photogate. T² against L uses bob rows with θ₀ ≤ 10°: slope 4π²/g. T against θ₀ shows how far large swings depart from T₀.

Lab

Blind hides the predicted periods and g from the fit; the photogate, the settings and the trace stay. The Mystery planet always hides its g.

Model: a point bob on a massless, inextensible string (or a thin uniform rod) about a frictionless pivot, integrated exactly enough (fourth order Runge–Kutta, 1 ms steps) that the photogate's 0.001 s readings are reliable. Air drag, when on, is a small force in proportion to speed. The photogate times every second pass of the bottom.
