Elevator: Apparent Weight

Stand on a bathroom scale in an elevator. The scale reads the normal force N, not your weight mg. It reads more while the elevator speeds up going up, less while it speeds up going down, and exactly mg at any constant speed.

Lab

Blind hides the scale reading, the N arrow and the scale graph, so the class can predict each stage of the ride first.

Model: the car's acceleration is a constant ±a while it starts and stops and zero while it cruises (real elevators ramp the acceleration up and down smoothly). The scale is massless and reads instantly, in kilograms as N/g. Cable snaps: one second of free fall, then the safety brakes stop the car at a constant deceleration of at least g, more if needed to stop above the pit.
