Equivalence and Tests of Gravity

Shut in a room with no windows, you cannot tell resting on Earth from riding a rocket that speeds up at g. Einstein built general relativity on that fact. Follow it to light that bends, check it against the 1919 eclipse, and watch it explain the slow turning of Mercury's orbit.
