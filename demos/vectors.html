River

The model and its formulas

vboat,shore = vboat,water + vwater,shore

More about this

The river is 60 m wide. The current is the same everywhere, or (Slower near the banks) zero at each bank and fastest mid river, u(y) = 4u y(W − y)/W², averaging 2u/3; a real river's profile is roughly like this. The boat keeps its heading and speed relative to the water, so its path curves when the current varies. The arrows at the left show the current across the river.

#Strings, mass and angleRing
More about this

Each string pulls the ring with its tension, which for a hanging mass at rest is its weight, F = mg with g = 9.81 m/s². Angles are counterclockwise from the 0° mark. The strings are massless, the ring is light and slides freely on the table, and every string is taken to point at the pin, ignoring the ring's few millimetres of play. Pulley friction lets each string's tension differ from its hanging weight by up to the amount set (given as the weight of that many grams; a chosen value, not a measured one), so the ring centers over a small range of masses and angles, as on a real table. At 0 the pulleys are ideal and the ring centers only when the weights cancel exactly.
