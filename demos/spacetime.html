Each tick on a worldline is one year on that clock, so wider spacing means a slower clock in this frame. The twin flies out at β and back at −β.

Layers

The faint hyperbolas are (ct)² − x² = ±1, ±4, ±9 and so on. A Lorentz boost slides every event along its hyperbola, so each frame's tick n lands where its axis crosses the n² hyperbola. That is why the primed ticks look stretched.

Controls and model

On the diagram: drag empty space to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. With the diagram focused, ← → ↑ ↓ move the selected event (Shift for bigger steps), [ ] switch events, Del removes it, N adds one, M switches the view, Space plays.

Model: exact special relativity in one space dimension with c = 1, so light covers one light year per year. All coordinates come from the Lorentz transformation. The twin's turnaround is treated as instantaneous.
