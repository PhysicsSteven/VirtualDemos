Vectors and Surfaces in Space

A space you can turn: vectors by their x, y and z components with their sum, dot product and cross product, a line and a plane and where they meet, the quadric surfaces with their traces, and a point in rectangular, cylindrical and spherical coordinates with its coordinate surfaces. The physics: torque r × F, the magnetic force qv × B, work F · d, a ray meeting a mirror and the potential of a point charge.

The formulas

a · b = axbx + ayby + azbz = |a||b| cos θ, a × b = (aybz − azby, azbx − axbz, axby − aybx), |a × b| = |a||b| sin θ

line r = P₀ + t d meets the plane n · (r − Q) = 0 at t = n · (Q − P₀)/(n · d); reflected direction d − 2(d · n̂)n̂

x = r sin θ cos φ, y = r sin θ sin φ, z = r cos θ; x = ρ cos φ, y = ρ sin φ, z = z

Every number is computed from the components in the boxes, exactly as the formulas say, and rounded only for display. Vectors with different units (r and F, v and B) are drawn to different scales, each named on the picture; their directions, and the direction of the cross product, are true. The proton's charge is e from CODATA 2022 (exact); the point charge uses the CODATA 2022 Coulomb constant with q = 1 nC.
