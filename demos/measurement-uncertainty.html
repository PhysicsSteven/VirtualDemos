Measurement Uncertainty

Measure g with a pendulum, and find out what your data can and cannot tell you. Time the swings by hand or with a photogate, time one swing or many, read the length to the right place or the wrong one, and release from a small angle or a big one. Random scatter shrinks when you repeat; a bias does not.
