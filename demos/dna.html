About the model

Coordinates. Every atom is placed from the A and B DNA fiber models of Arnott and coworkers, refined against X ray fiber diffraction: cylindrical coordinates (r, φ, z) for each atom of each nucleotide, as tabulated in the fd_helix routine of the Nucleic Acid Builder (D. A. Case, AmberTools/AmberClassic), which cites Fuller et al., J. Mol. Biol. 12, 60 (1965) and Arnott, Campbell Smith and Chandrasekaran, CRC Handbook of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, 3rd ed., Nucleic Acids vol. II, 411 (1976). Pair k is turned by k times the twist and raised by k times the rise; strand 2 is the image of strand 1 under a twofold axis through each pair, which makes the strands antiparallel. B form: 3.38 Å and 36.0° per pair, 10 pairs per turn. A form: 2.56 Å and 32.7°, 11 pairs per turn.

Approximations. The helix is uniform: every step has the same rise and twist, and every A·T or G·C pair the same shape. Real DNA varies from step to step with sequence (roll, twist, propeller twist, groove width), and crystal structures in the Protein Data Bank, such as the Dickerson dodecamer (PDB 1BNA), differ from this model by up to a few ångströms. Hydrogen atoms are not placed, so hydrogen bonds are shown between donor and acceptor atoms (N or O), and space filling spheres are heavy atoms only. Z DNA is not offered: the fiber table used here has no Z form. The cartoon is a drawing aid: a tube through the phosphorus atoms and a slab for each base.

Other numbers. Space filling radii are Bondi's van der Waals radii (J. Phys. Chem. 68, 441, 1964): C 1.70, N 1.55, O 1.52, P 1.80 Å. Molar mass sums the nucleotide residues (dA 313.21, dC 289.18, dG 329.21, dT 304.20 g/mol) minus 61.96 g/mol per strand for strands with no 5′ phosphate, as in this model. Tm uses the unified nearest neighbor parameters of SantaLucia, PNAS 95, 1460 (1998), with the salt correction ΔS += 0.368 (N − 1) ln[Na+], total strand concentration 0.5 µM, and no magnesium. It is an estimate for a two state transition of short duplexes.
