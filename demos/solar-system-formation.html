Building a Solar System

Watch a slowly turning cloud of gas and dust collapse into the young Sun and a spinning disk, then follow it all the way to planets. The whole story runs each step for one cloud and zooms from the cloud's scale down to the planets'; Follow keeps one planet's material in view. The four stages are labs for each step: the collapse, the frost line, how planetesimals grow, and how early ones melt so their iron sinks to a core.
