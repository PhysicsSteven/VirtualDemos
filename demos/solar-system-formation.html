Building a Solar System

A spinning cloud of gas and dust falls in on itself and flattens into a disk around the young Sun. Near the Sun the disk is hot and only metal and rock can be solid; far out it is cold enough for ice. Watch the solids clump into planetesimals, the biggest beyond the frost line grab gas, and early ones melt so their iron sinks to a core.
