    Key

    Convective cells are illustrative: their extent comes from MESA's mixing_type, and their relative speed from log_conv_vel (compressed so slow and fast regions both show). The loop pattern itself is drawn, not computed.

    Glow marks zones with εnuc above 10 erg g⁻¹ s⁻¹ and above 10⁻⁴ of the moment's peak. Channel shares use MESA's pp, cno and tri_alpha columns; any remainder is other reactions.
