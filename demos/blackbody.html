Layers

Rayleigh-Jeans is the classical prediction, 2ckT/λ⁴. It matches at long wavelengths but grows without limit at short ones: the ultraviolet catastrophe. Planck fixed it by letting light carry energy only in quanta hf.

Why no green stars?

More about this

A Planck curve is broad. When its peak sits in the green, near 5000 to 6000 K, it still sends out nearly as much red and blue light, and the eye sees the mix as white. No temperature gives a spectrum narrow enough to look green, so the color scale runs red, orange, yellow, white, blue and skips green entirely.

The model and its formulas

Model: ideal blackbodies. Real stars have absorption lines and are classified by them; T here is the effective temperature. Colors use an analytic fit to the CIE 1931 observer (Wyman, Sloan and Shirley 2013) and sRGB with a D65 white point, so a 6500 K body shows as white. Each swatch is scaled to full brightness; your screen and eye adaptation change what you see.
